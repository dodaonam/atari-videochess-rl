# Risk Register

**Canonical source for:** project risks and response plans. A risk is a possible failure, not evidence that it has occurred. Blockers are maintained separately in `.agents/state/BLOCKERS.md`; current progress is in `.agents/state/PROJECT_STATE.yaml`.

| ID | Risk | Initial severity | Status | Planned response / evidence needed |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| RSK-001 | ALE board extraction or state synchronization is unreliable, especially while Atari is thinking. | High | Open; mitigated on recorded M1 profiles | M1 demonstrated phase-specific RGB checks, safe desynchronization stops, and legal multi-turn transitions. Preserve the last verified board and validate broader profiles/special moves separately; see [M1/G2 final verification](../../.agents/evidence/M1-G2-final-verification-20261016.md). |
| RSK-002 | Atari Video Chess and `python-chess` differ in rules, turn behavior, or terminal conditions. | High | Open; partially tested | M1 observed ordinary legal turns and a genuine mode-4 loss terminal, and reset-tested modes 0–4. Special moves, Atari draw-rule parity, terminal-board rendering and gameplay terminal behavior in other modes remain unverified. Keep source, synthetic-test, and runtime evidence separate. |
| RSK-003 | Stockfish evaluations have a different perspective/scale/meaning from Double DQN Q-values. | High | Open; design concern | M3–M5: record perspective and target transform, unit-test signs/scales, verify reward alignment before TD updates. |
| RSK-004 | Sparse terminal rewards or unstable updates cause RL to regress the pretraining baseline. | High | Open; not measured | Keep pretraining and RL checkpoints; compare under the same benchmark; report regressions honestly. |
| RSK-005 | Colab free GPU availability or session duration is insufficient for planned workloads. | Medium | Open; not checked | M0: inspect current quota/runtime capability; checkpoint/resume and persistent artifacts; no paid compute without authorization. |

| RSK-007 | Simulator skill does not transfer to actual Atari play. | High | Open; not measured | Keep simulator and Atari results distinct; require M1 before substantial learning and closed-loop M6 evaluation. |
| RSK-008 | Source-described ALE defaults, RAM addressing, mode mapping, or ROM availability differ from the installed version. | Medium | Open; partially checked | M1 recorded ALE-Py 0.11.2, ROM hash, profiles, exposed modes/difficulties and wrapper behavior against the installed runtime. Recheck version-sensitive defaults, RAM use and mode behavior if the runtime changes; gameplay terminal evidence covers deterministic mode 4 only. See [ALE environment findings](../03-technical/ALE_ENVIRONMENT.md) and [M1/G2 final verification](../../.agents/evidence/M1-G2-final-verification-20261016.md). |

Review [MDP_SPEC.md](../03-technical/MDP_SPEC.md) and proposed [ADR-0002](decisions/ADR-0002-chess-mdp-action-and-perspective.md). |

Do not mark a risk resolved based only on a proposed mitigation. Link verified findings, test evidence, or an ADR when the underlying uncertainty is actually resolved.
