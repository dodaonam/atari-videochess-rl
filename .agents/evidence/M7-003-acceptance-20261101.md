# M7-003 Acceptance — Elo Reportability

**Disposition:** **Elo not reported: calibrated pool/design not approved or unavailable.**
**Status:** Complete; evidence for acceptance criterion `M7-03`.
**Basis:** `M7-EVAL/1.0.0`, §7, plus the raw-derived M7 Option B evaluation in [M7-002 analysis](M7-002-acceptance-20261101.md).

No rating value or rating interval is reported. No opponent campaign was added, and no M4 imitation, M5 simulator score, Atari move-execution rate, or M7 single-opponent score was converted into Elo.

| Required Elo design element | Status in the available evidence | Why it is unmet |
|---|---|---|
| Calibrated opponent pool | Not approved or available | M7 evaluates against only the built-in VideoChess opponent. RandomLegal and SimpleHeuristic comparisons in M5 are simulator evidence, not a calibrated Atari pool. No owner-approved pool of at least three calibrated opponents exists. |
| Rating anchor | Not approved | No opponent rating source/date or defensible fixed anchor was authorized. No 1500 assumption is made. |
| Opponent diversity | Absent | M7 has one Atari opponent; historical M6 uses the same single opponent under distinct policies/profiles. Combining those does not create a calibrated diverse pool. |
| Frozen Elo match schedule | Absent | The approved 90-cell M7 schedule varies policy and shared ALE seed only; all games are White. It is not a balanced opponent × color schedule. No separate Elo schedule was authorized. |
| Minimum terminal games | Not met | The protocol calls for at least 30 ALE-terminal games per policy/opponent/color cell. M7 yielded between 0 and 10 terminal games per policy against its single opponent, with 30 of 90 attempts incomplete overall. M5 simulator games are not substitutable. |
| Color balance | Not met | M7 evaluates White only. There are no paired Black games under the same calibrated opponent/profile. |
| Rating model | Not approved for this population | Although the protocol names a fixed-anchor maximum-likelihood Elo model on the standard 400-point logistic scale, its anchor, match schedule, opponent uncertainty, rating period, and draw treatment were not instantiated and approved. |
| Confidence interval design | Not estimable for Elo | The required 95% match-block bootstrap must preserve evaluation- and training-seed clusters. The available M7 outcomes are heavily censored and have only three training-seed pairs; there is no calibrated multi-opponent match set to resample. The paired M7 performance intervals are accordingly withheld where censoring prevents valid pairs. |

The protocol also states that the 30-terminal-games-per-cell threshold is not itself a power guarantee and prefers a formal power calculation. Neither a power plan nor an Elo-specific evaluation budget was approved. Adding one would expand the authorized scope.

## Acceptance

M7-003 meets `M7-03`: reportability was checked against the approved conditions and the result is explicitly **Elo not reported**. No unsupported rating, 1500 baseline, unapproved Stockfish use, or additional match schedule is introduced. G4 acceptance remains an independent PM decision.
