## 1. Finding and root cause

At the original reviewed HEAD, `docs/07-delivery/RELEASE_DISPOSITION.md` combined the historical corrective r4/r5 release ID with the accepted final r6/r7 dataset manifest SHA. Its M3 teacher row identified corrective teacher-r2 and the historical dataset binding but did not mark that generation as historical or inventory accepted final teacher-r1.

The visible defect is a generation-mixed inventory row and an incomplete teacher inventory. The checked records do not establish how those stale identifiers entered the prose; this report does not speculate about authoring history. The defect was in delivery documentation and provenance representation. It is not evidence that the accepted M3 artifacts, their source records, or any M7 results were incorrect.
