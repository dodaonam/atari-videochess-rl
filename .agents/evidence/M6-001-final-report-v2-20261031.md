## 8. Limitations and handover

- Every terminal M5 game in v2 was a loss. The sample does not establish policy strength or a Warm/Scratch winner.
- Nine of 20 attempts were incomplete: five opponent-reply timeouts, two source-selection uncertainty stops, and two target-navigation uncertainty stops. They were not converted to draws or replaced.
- All observed attempts were White-side, numeric mode 4, difficulty 0. Black-side ALE support and human-readable mode/difficulty semantics remain unverified.
- The terminal Atari screen can clear. Each attempt preserves the last independently RGB-verified FEN and uses ALE flags/reward for outcome classification.
- The 500,000-frame setting is an intentional, supported horizon extension for the pinned runtime; it must be reverified if the Gymnasium/ALE package changes.
- M5's accepted RSK-014 historical CLI-byte limitation remains accepted and unchanged. V2 provenance is complete for its own source, config, policies, runtime, ROM, schedule, ledger, traces, and manifests.
- V2 reuses v1 ALE seeds under a changed horizon; it is not an independent replication. No Elo claim is made. M7 remains unauthorized.
